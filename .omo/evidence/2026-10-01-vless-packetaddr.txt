Focused unit coverage:
- packetaddr magic destination is emitted in the VLESS UDP request header.
- packetaddr uses port-then-address framing for IPv4/IPv6 destinations.
- packetaddr response frames decode the destination and payload.
- IPv6 packetaddr encoding is explicitly covered.
- empty packetaddr segments are rejected, matching the V2Ray framing contract.
- FQDN packetaddr destinations are rejected because packetaddr serializes IP
  address families only.
- packet-encoding:packetaddr and the legacy packet alias resolve to packetaddr.
- packet-encoding:packetaddr cannot be combined with xudp:true.

Compatibility note:
The V2Ray interop test uses encryption:none because V2Ray 5.41.0 does not
implement Xray-native VLESS Encryption. Native-encryption packetaddr is covered
by the same framing codec/unit tests but is not claimed as cross-implementation
crypto interop. XHTTP packetaddr cross-implementation interop is also not
claimed because the available V2Ray target does not provide the required
server-side XHTTP transport.
